Inside Vega Router Current work & what’s next +

Rules you can read.

Vega Router brings local and cloud models into a workflow with explicit policy and a record of each decision. v1 runs daily on the founder’s Mac. The open-core release is planned for fall 2026.

On the drawing board.

Dispatch Desk, the native Mac app, is in design. Its planned split view would show what stays local and what goes out before a job runs. The interface above illustrates the idea, not a released app.
